DELIVERY & PAYMENT

Simple delivery across the UAE & GCC

UAE

UAE Delivery

AED 30 delivery across the UAE. Western Region delivery is AED 50. Standard delivery is within up to 4 days.

COD

Cash on Delivery

Cash on delivery is available in the UAE only. COD orders are prepared in one day and delivered the following day, normally within 2 days.

GCC

GCC Delivery

Delivery is available across GCC countries starting from AED 85. Estimated delivery time is 4–7 days.

Returns & Inspection Policy

  • You may inspect the product when the courier arrives and before paying the courier.
  • If the product arrives damaged or you do not wish to accept it after inspection, you may return it immediately without paying the courier.
  • After accepting the order, return requests must be made within 48 hours of delivery.
  • Returned products must be in the same condition in which they were received, without damage, modification or signs of use.
  • Custom-made or personalized products cannot be returned after acceptance unless they arrive damaged or materially different from the agreed specification.

PETG Material & Heat Notice

All FIX3D.AE products are 3D printed using PETG. PETG heat resistance can vary depending on the product design, load and conditions. To prevent deformation or damage, avoid exposing products to temperatures above approximately 65–80°C, direct high heat, flames, or prolonged exposure inside very hot vehicles.

Order Policy

Because our products are 3D printed, minor layer lines or small cosmetic variations may occur and are part of the manufacturing process. Product colors may vary slightly depending on material and screen display. Delivery estimates may be affected by courier delays or circumstances outside FIX3D.AE's control. By placing an order, the customer confirms the product details, quantity and delivery information.
